1

Air comes in from every side

The perforated lower body draws room air in all the way round, so it works whichever way it faces on your desk.

2

A HEPA filter catches what's in it

Inside the body sits a pleated HEPA filter. Smoke, dust and pet dander stay in the pleats instead of the room.

3

Clean air leaves through the top

The fan pushes filtered air up through the grille, carrying negative ions with it.

Drag to turn it. Tap the top to switch it on or off.
